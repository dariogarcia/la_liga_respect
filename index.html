What is this?

Respect Rank tracks how La Liga managers behave towards referees in post-match press quotes. Every game, each coach's comments are collected from public press coverage, quoted verbatim with a link to the publisher, and graded for respect.

Scoring

3 · Respectful 1 · Neutral 0 · Disrespectful 3 · Respectful silence
  • Quotes are graded by an LLM against the referee-related comment; a low-confidence heuristic fallback marks the row when the LLM is unavailable.
  • A game where the coach spoke to the press but never mentioned the referee counts as confirmed respectful silence (3 points).
  • If no coverage at all can be found within the search window, the game is presumed respectful silence (3 points) — the burden of proof is on finding disrespect, not on proving innocence.

The two rankings

Season Total — each coach accumulates points from every game they coached (including 3-point silences). This is the pure measure of respectfulness across the season; a coach switching clubs mid-season gets one row per club.

Head-to-Head — every game becomes a duel between the two coaches on the touchline that day: the more respectful of the two takes 3 points and the other 0, and a tie gives 1 point each. It reads like a league table built from respect instead of goals, and rewards showing up your counterpart.

Limitations

  • This leaderboard is generated by an automated system: quotes may be misattributed and scores are subjective.
  • Press coverage in Spanish sport media is the only source; a comment not reported anywhere is invisible to the system.
  • Click any row in the Ranking tab to see the underlying quotes, scores and sources, and judge for yourself.
