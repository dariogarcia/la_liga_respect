# LLM Grading Mechanism: Respect Level

This document details the logic and the specific prompt used by the LLM to grade coach comments.

## 1. The Grading Logic
The system uses a **Zero-Shot Classification** approach with a strictly defined rubric. The LLM acts as an impartial judge specializing in sports diplomacy and linguistic analysis.

### Scoring Rubric
- **3 Points (Respectful)**: 
    - Coach acknowledges the difficulty of the job.
    - Explicitly states that the referee's decisions are final/respectable.
    - Avoids blaming the referee for the match outcome.
- **1 Point (Neutral)**:
    - Mentions the referee but without emotional charge.
    - Expresses a difference of opinion on a decision without using aggressive language.
    - The tone is professional and objective.
- **0 Points (Disrespectful)**:
    - Accuses the referee of bias or incompetence.
    - Uses sarcastic or aggressive language.
    - Claims the match was "stolen" or "robbed".
    - Calls for sanctions against the officiating crew.

## 2. The System Prompt
When a quote is sent to the LLM, the following system prompt is used:

> **System Prompt:**
> You are an expert analyst of sports psychology and a specialist in La Liga football. Your task is to evaluate the level of respect a coach shows toward the referee in a post-game interview.
>
> **Instructions:**
> 1. Analyze the provided text for tone, sentiment, and specific keywords.
> 2. Assign a score based on the following strict rubric:
>    - **3 (Respectful)**: Acknowledges the referee's difficulty, avoids blaming them for the result, or praises the officiating.
>    - **1 (Neutral)**: Mentions the referee neutrally or expresses disagreement without aggression or accusations of bias.
>    - **0 (Disrespectful)**: Openly criticizes the referee, implies bias, uses aggressive language, or calls for sanctions.
> 3. You MUST NOT assign any value other than 0, 1, or 3.
> 4. Provide your answer in JSON format with the following keys:
>    - `score`: The assigned point value (integer).
>    - `reasoning`: A brief explanation of why this score was chosen, referencing specific phrases from the text.
>
> **Text to evaluate:**
> "[COACH_QUOTE]"

## 3. Handling Edge Cases
- **Silence**: If a coach does not mention the referee at all, the quote is ignored and not graded (it does not count as a neutral 1).
- **Sarcasm**: The LLM is instructed to detect sarcasm. A statement like "The referee did a *fantastic* job of ruining our game" is graded as **0**, not **3**.
- **Multi-sentence quotes**: If a coach is respectful in one sentence but aggressive in another, the **lowest score** (most disrespectful) takes precedence.

## 4. Competitive Mode Derivation
The Competitive Leaderboard does not use a separate prompt. Instead, it takes the absolute scores generated by the process above for two coaches in the same game:
- `Coach A (3) vs Coach B (1)` $\rightarrow$ Coach A wins (3 pts), Coach B loses (0 pts).
- `Coach A (1) vs Coach B (1)` $\rightarrow$ Draw (1 pt each).
- `Coach A (0) vs Coach B (0)` $\rightarrow$ Draw (1 pt each).
